# Brand assets

The Apple, Tesla and NVIDIA monochrome brand marks in public/brands are sourced from Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org/), repository https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners. These assets remain available locally for first-party views. The public market widget manages its own provider-supplied logos.

JPMorganChase: official website icon from https://www.jpmorganchase.com/etc.clientlibs/cws/clientlibs/clientlib-base/resources/jpmc/images/jpmc-favicon-152.png.
ExxonMobil: official investor relations icon linked at https://investor.exxonmobil.com, hosted at https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_14a89599fd1b18d59cc379f8fa0633eb/exxonmobil/files/theme/images/favicons/android-chrome-192x192.png.
SPY: issuer State Street's official icon, https://www.ssga.com/favicon.ico.

The public landing explorer now embeds TradingView's official Screener, including its provider-supplied logos and branding. It no longer requests Elbstream logos or the backend's public logo endpoint. The local assets above remain available for other first-party views; brand ownership is unchanged. See [public-market-explorer.md](public-market-explorer.md) for the current integration and attribution.
